How Much Does Stump Grinding Cost in Middleton?

Pricing is straightforward: roughly $3 to $5 per inch of ground-level diameter, a minimum around $150, and a discount on each added stump. Bigger legacy trees cost more simply because there's more wood to grind. We'll walk the yard with you and give a firm, free quote before any work starts.
